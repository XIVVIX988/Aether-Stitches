# Æther Stitches — Product Catalogue Intake

**Purpose:** Collect verified product information for a future catalogue. This is a working form for the business owner; it does not add products to the website.

## How to use this document

1. Start with the **Product inventory summary** below. Add one row for each product you want considered.
2. Copy the **Product entry** section once for each product. If there are no variants, mark **No variants** and leave the repeatable variant entries blank.
3. Fill in what you know. Use `NOT DECIDED` when a business choice is pending, `UNKNOWN` when you do not know a fact, and `NOT APPLICABLE` when a field does not apply. Do not guess or leave a blank that could be mistaken for “not applicable.”
4. Enter prices only when approved by Æther Stitches. Keep KES, UGX, and TZS separate. Do not convert one currency to another.
5. Add image filenames or references when genuine product photos are ready. Do not create or edit photos to fill a gap.
6. Use the product checklist at the end before handing an entry over for catalogue preparation.

**Required** means needed before the product can be considered ready for publication. **Optional** means useful where known. If a required fact is not ready, mark it with one of the values above; do not invent a replacement. Publication approval and readiness are separate: this form records them but does not publish anything.

## Product handover checklist

Before handing this product entry over, check:

- [ ] English product name and short description are factual and approved.
- [ ] Swahili name and short description are supplied and reviewed, or the English-only exception has owner approval.
- [ ] Category uses the owner's current decision; unresolved taxonomy is marked `NOT DECIDED`.
- [ ] Product type and current sale availability are recorded separately and consistently.
- [ ] Variants are either clearly marked “No variants” or each real variant has its own entry.
- [ ] Every price is owner-approved, labelled with its market currency, and linked to the product or exact variant it covers.
- [ ] No exchange rate, price, stock count, dimension, translation, option, image, or policy has been guessed.
- [ ] Stock quantity is linked to the product or to each separately stocked variant, without competing duplicate quantities.
- [ ] Market stock is marked shared, market-specific, unknown, or not decided; no technical inventory decision was forced.
- [ ] Image filenames/references match genuine photos that Æther may use, or image readiness is marked honestly.
- [ ] Processing-time notes do not imply a transit time or an unconfirmed promise.
- [ ] Publication approval and remaining owner decisions are recorded.

## Collection-wide review

Before the collected entries move to the next project stage, confirm that:

- Each product has its own entry and appears once in the summary inventory.
- Every unknown or undecided fact is visibly marked for follow-up.
- Prices have not been converted between KES, UGX, and TZS.
- The proposed shared-stock default has not been treated as approved without owner confirmation.
- The unresolved category choices have not been settled by assumption.
- No unapproved product content or imagery has been added to the website.

This intake document gathers source information only. It does not publish products, create catalogue data files, reserve inventory, or implement website features.
