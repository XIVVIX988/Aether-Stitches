# Home and catalog surface brief

## Scope and visitor mode

Persuade: help everyday shoppers discover a bag and choose ready-made or custom. This phase builds the homepage and category catalogue only; live checkout, payments, freight quotes, returns administration, order tracking, and stock administration are later work.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

- Audience: everyday personal-use shoppers in Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania.
- Job: find a relevant bag category, understand service terms, then browse or begin a custom enquiry.
- Primary action: explore the collection; custom enquiry is the secondary action.
- Proof/content: Aether’s supplied brand commitment, material list, timelines, delivery rules, and lifetime repair promise. No customer reviews or actual products/prices/photos are supplied yet.
- Constraints: English and Swahili; KSh, UGX, TSh; use the supplied logo and gold/off-white/black identity; do not invent product photos, SKUs, prices, testimonials, or unsupported claims.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A familiar, carefully made storefront makes it easy to choose between a ready-made bag and a custom piece. Refuse the generic dropship catalogue by grounding the experience in Aether’s real service terms and craft commitments.

**OWN-WORLD:** Established East African bag maker. Use the supplied gold mark with black and warm off-white; restrained type and direct labels; the logo and real bag photography should carry the identity when photos arrive.

**STORY:** Shoppers see what Aether makes, understand delivery and timing, then explore categories or begin a custom enquiry. Market selection updates the country-specific delivery message; language selection changes site copy.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Familiar storefront: compact header with category links and market/language controls; left-side offer and clear “Explore the collection” action; right-side honest product-image placeholder until Aether supplies photography. Categories and service terms begin immediately below.

**FORM:** User-selected category standard, built code-first. The named references—Urban Ranch Kenya, Home 254, Denri—set the craft/experience bar. Chosen standing exit: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/canon.html`. Seed reference: `seed:9c6d29cc18663074`.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction and risk

The market selector switches between Kenya/KSh, Uganda/UGX, and Tanzania/TSh and updates delivery guidance. The language control switches English/Swahili. Risk: real prices, inventory, photography, WhatsApp details, and regional gateway are still missing; make that absence clear and keep the preview out of production checkout.

## Unresolved decisions

Actual catalog data and prices; regional payment gateway; shipment/transit times and final freight quoting; precise return and repair conditions; contact details; final custom deposit and quote terms; production platform for checkout and admin.
