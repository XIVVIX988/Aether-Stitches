## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS prototype first; later commerce and administration platform remains undecided.

## Users

Primary users are everyday consumers buying bags for personal use in Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania. Secondary users include customers seeking custom-made pieces and like-minded creators or brands considering collaboration.

## Product Purpose

Æther Stitches sells ready-made bags and accepts custom commissions across East Africa. The website should help shoppers explore products, understand materials and prices, place ready-made orders, request custom quotes, and contact the brand. Success means a clear, dependable path from discovery to purchase or custom enquiry.

## Positioning

Æther Stitches combines ready-made choices with customer-led customization: “You dream it, we stitch it!” Its commitment is to thoughtful craftsmanship, honest representation, customer-centred service, dependable follow-through, and personal expression.

## Operating Context

- Core markets are Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania.
- KSh is the primary currency; UGX and TSh are also required.
- Kenya delivery is complimentary. Uganda/Tanzania freight is quoted manually unless a calculated-rate option is chosen later.
- Kenya payment is M-Pesa. Uganda/Tanzania payment options are undecided and require a regional gateway.
- Ready-made processing is 3 working days. Custom production is 7 working days after it begins, once schedule, quote, and deposit are confirmed.
- Return requests may be submitted within 7 calendar days.
- Lifetime repairs are promised subject to conditions; intentionally inflicted damage is excluded.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Ready-made sales and custom-order enquiries are both in scope.
- Product categories: backpacks, travel duffels, sling bags, tote bags, wallets, caps, crossbody bags, purses, and handbags.
- Materials: PU leather, genuine leather, canvas, and kitenge.
- Prices should be shown as ranges when appropriate. Product status must control purchasing: out of stock disables purchase; made to order accepts an enquiry; coming soon is visible without checkout.
- Admin users must later be able to edit prices and stock quantities without editing code.
- Future commerce/admin requirements include separate ready-made and custom flows, deposit/balance tracking, manual cross-border freight quotes, return request management, order status tracking, and admin notifications for orders, quotations, and returns.
- Delivery and production timelines must be visible before payment.
- Payment and shipping integrations are not yet implemented. Regional payment options and product/catalog data remain open.
- Site content must be available in English and Swahili.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: Æther Stitches.
- Tagline: “ÆTHER STITCHES — YOU DREAM IT, WE STITCH IT!”
- Palette commitments: gold from the supplied logo, off-white, and black.
- Logo: `Assets/Æther Stitches Logo.png`.
- The user supplied “Why Choose Æther Stitches?” copy emphasizing value, thoughtful craft, honest product representation, customer-centred service, dependable interactions, and personal expression.
- The user provided Urban Ranch Kenya as the primary inspiration and Home 254 and Denri as supporting references. Learn from their public information architecture and commerce patterns; do not copy their assets or language.
- The user chose the familiar storefront/category standard for the first page and catalogue. The three named reference sites are the craft and experience quality bar.
- Collaboration with like-minded creators and brands is part of the business.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product and workshop photographs will be supplied later; do not invent product photos, catalog prices, product claims, or customer reviews.
- Product names, SKUs, actual prices/ranges, availability, options, dimensions, photos, and stock quantities are not yet provided.

## Product Principles

1. Be transparent about product, price, timing, delivery charges, and custom-order terms.
2. Keep ready-made purchasing distinct from quoted custom production.
3. Earn trust through useful information and dependable customer care.
4. Give customers room to express their needs through materials and customization.
5. Do not claim features, inventory, reviews, or capabilities without evidence from Æther Stitches.
